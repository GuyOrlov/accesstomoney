Accessibility statement

Access to Money is being designed to work with keyboard navigation, screen magnification, screen readers, high contrast, responsive layouts and plain-language content. We also plan BSL-supported versions of key guidance.

Tell us about a barrier

Email hello@accesstomoney.co.uk. Please tell us the page and what went wrong.

Technical target

We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 AA. This early version should be audited before a public accessibility claim is made.
